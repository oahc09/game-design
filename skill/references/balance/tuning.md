# Balance and difficulty

Freeze a baseline, parameter ranges, target metric bands, player versions, and a multi-seed set. Separate tuning seeds from held-out evaluation seeds. Use paired seeds/profile budgets for every candidate and keep invalid configurations in the attempted denominator.

Return measured before/after vectors, parameter sensitivity, interaction effects, confidence limitations, and Pareto tradeoffs. Flag dead parameters, cliffs, dominant strategies, and low skill expression. Do not recommend unmeasured values as established improvements; label them proposals.

A competent bot is a named policy, not an ordinary human. Difficulty combines observed policy performance and structural metrics; human difficulty/fairness needs calibration. Large bot gaps may show policy sensitivity rather than genuine depth. One favorable seed cannot support population claims.
