# Quality vector

Keep Validity, Solvability, Difficulty, Skill Discrimination, Decision Quality, Interaction, Diversity, and Robustness separate. Each dimension declares applicability, metric implementation/version, measurement, sample size, evidence, and confidence or limitation.

Use completion distributions and structural evidence for difficulty; best-versus-median action value and forced-action share for decision quality; parameter and seed sweeps for robustness. A metric name in a spec does not mean its measurement exists. Missing and not applicable are not zero. Use Wilson intervals for binomial rates and disclose approximation/instability for continuous summaries. Do not interpret benchmark tasks as a random sample of all possible games.
