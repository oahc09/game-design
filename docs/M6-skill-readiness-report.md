## Known limitations

The intent classifier is a reviewable lexical heuristic, not a language-understanding model. It prioritizes the first detected intent and combines explicit secondary imperative clauses; implicit or ambiguous work still needs review. Fixtures exercise meaningful distinct problems but remain author-created development tests. Receipt validation checks structure and status, not the truth of arbitrary externally supplied receipts. The built-in toolkit adapters provide actual measured receipts.

Campaign selection requires a measured and annotated content pool. Raw generator candidates do not establish mechanic mastery, twist semantics, or human learning. Balance QA now evaluates the selected recommendation and identifies the exact checked spec hash.
